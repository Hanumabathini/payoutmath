# PayoutMath — verified fee methodology (25 November 2026)

**Scope:** US-based sellers, USD. Examples are estimates, not statements of a seller's exact payout.

## Verified official reference pages
- eBay: https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/selling/start-selling-on-ebay/seller-fees — percentage includes item, shipping and applicable tax; per-order fees and category-specific rates must be modeled separately.
- Poshmark: https://poshmark.com/posh_guide/get_started_on_poshmark — US flat fee below $15, percentage otherwise.
- Mercari: https://www.mercari.com/us/help_center/article/2517 — seller fees; read the effective-date conditions.
- Depop: https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges — US processing; boosted listings: https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/9253129255569-Boosted-Listings-Policy .
- Etsy: https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/ — transaction fee, listing fee, Offsite Ads, bank-country processing and other adjustments.
- Whatnot: https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4847069165965-Whatnot-seller-fees and https://www.whatnot.com/seller/fees — **tiered from September 21, 2026**. The commission shown in Seller Hub supersedes generic estimates.
- PayPal: https://www.paypal.com/us/business/paypal-business-fees and https://www.paypal.com/us/webapps/mpp/merchant-fees — fee depends on payment service.

## What the calculator MUST NOT claim
- An 8% Whatnot commission is *not* universal in late 2026. The seller's four-week sales tier and category determine the rate. Default category rates are example baselines only. Limited-time above-$1,500 discounts must never be applied without current verification.
- Cross-marketplace rankings are illustrative: a user cannot assume equal sale prices, shipping labels or fees across every marketplace.
- Processing fee, total seller fees, estimated payout, profit, net margin, and ROI are different concepts. Each must be labeled.
- No guaranteed affiliate commission or earnings; Crosslist affiliate participation is optional and requires approval.

## Required future inputs for exactness
US seller category; store subscription status; seller-level Whatnot commission; seller shipping cost; buyer-paid shipping; seller coupon/discount; sales tax applied to processing fees; promotion/boost; returns and refunds; currency; optional listing fee; actual platform statement. If input missing, say `estimate`.

## Verification tests
Boundary tests at Poshmark $15, eBay $10 per-order fee, eBay category thresholds, Etsy Offsite Ads caps; Whatnot Seller Hub rate override and 0% promo exclusion; zero prices; refunds/negative profit; phone 390 px and desktop 1440 px; no unpublished rates.
